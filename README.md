Project 

Hospital Management System

## Project Overview

The Hospital Management System is an Excel-based business management solution developed to organize and analyze patient records, doctors and departments, admissions and ward occupancy, appointments and treatments, billing and payments, and pharmacy drug inventory.

The system was designed to replace scattered manual records with a structured system that makes daily hospital operations easier to record, monitor, and analyze.

## Problem Statement

Hospitals handle a large number of daily records involving patients, doctors, departments, wards, appointments, treatments, admissions, prescriptions, drug stock, and billing methods.

When these records are managed manually, healthcare facilities may experience difficulties such as:

- Inaccurate patient and billing records
- Difficulty tracking admissions and bed occupancy
- Errors in calculating revenue and outstanding payments
- Difficulty monitoring drug stock levels
- Poor payment records
- Difficulty retrieving previous appointments or treatment history
- Limited visibility into overall hospital performance

## Problem Solving

The system addresses these challenges by providing a structured and automated approach to hospital management.

### Patient Management

A Patients database records important patient information, including:

- Patient ID
- Name
- Gender
- State
- Admission and treatment history

### Doctor & Department Management

Doctor and department records track staffing and capacity across the hospital, including:

- Doctor ID and Name
- Department assigned
- Beds allocated per department

The hospital currently runs **5 departments** (Cardiology, Neurology, Oncology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics) staffed by **10 doctors**, with each doctor carrying an evenly balanced caseload of appointments.

### Appointment & Treatment Tracking

Each appointment is logged with an Appointment ID, assigned doctor, and treatment type — covering services from Surgery and Consultation to Lab Tests, Scans, and Vaccinations. This makes it possible to see which services are most in demand and how appointment load is distributed across doctors.

### Admission & Ward Management

Admissions are tracked by Ward (A–E), with bed availability and occupancy calculated automatically. Out of **100 hospital beds**, the system currently reports an occupancy rate of **39%**, giving a clear, real-time read on available capacity.

### Billing & Payment Tracking

The system records billing amounts, payments received, and outstanding balances, broken down by payment method:

- Cash
- POS
- Transfer

This makes it easy to see total revenue, how much has been collected, and how much remains outstanding.

### Pharmacy & Drug Inventory

Drug stock is tracked by type and status — **Available**, **Low Stock**, or **Out of Stock** — alongside sales volume per drug. This flags reorder priorities before a shortage affects patient care.

### Data Accuracy

Structured tables, PivotTables, and data validation help reduce incorrect data entry and maintain consistency across patient, billing, and inventory records.

The system can be used to analyze:

- Total patients, doctors, and departments
- Bed occupancy and ward capacity
- Revenue, payments collected, and outstanding bills
- Appointment volume by doctor and treatment type
- Drug stock status and highest-selling drugs
- Payment method distribution
- Patient demographics (gender and location)

## Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how Excel can be used to build a practical hospital management system that combines data entry, automation, reporting, and analysis.

It provides a structured way to manage hospital records and transform raw operational data into useful, decision-ready business information — such as flagging that 64% of billed revenue is still outstanding, that bed occupancy sits at just 39% (leaving room for more admissions), and that two drug lines need urgent reordering.
